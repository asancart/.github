<h1 align="center">Asancart · آسان‌کارت</h1>

<p align="center"><b>Your assistant for automated peer-to-peer card payments.</b></p>

<p align="center" dir="rtl">دستیار شما برای پرداخت خودکار کارت به کارت</p>

---

Asancart lets small online stores in Iran take card-to-card (Shetab) payments the way they would through a payment gateway. Each order gets an amount with a unique rial suffix. An Android app on the shop owner's phone reads the bank's deposit SMS, parses it on the device and reports the deposit. Asancart matches it to the order and marks it paid.

- No payer account needed. The payer pays from any bank card.
- SMS text, balances and credentials never leave the phone.
- Plugins and an API for online stores.

**Status:** in development. Not open for sign-up yet.

## Public repositories

| Repository | What |
|---|---|
| [android](https://github.com/asancart/android) | Open-source Android SMS listener. Parses bank deposit SMS on the phone and reports them to Asancart or any backend that implements the Device API |
